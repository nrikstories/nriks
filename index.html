Questions

Before you share.

Who can share a story?

Any Kannadiga who works or has worked outside India, whatever the role. A nurse in Kuwait, a site engineer in Riyadh and a CTO in California are equally welcome.

Will my phone number or email be published?

No. They are used only by our team to contact you. Only what you approve in the final draft is published.

Can I mention my employer?

Yes, if your employer's social media policy allows it. Do not share confidential information. If unsure, leave the organisation field blank and we will describe it generally.

Is there a fee?

Featuring options are explained on the WhatsApp call before anything is published or charged. Submitting this form is free and commits you to nothing.

How do I remove my story or data?

Message us on WhatsApp. We take down the LinkedIn post and delete your submission data.

Admin sign-in

Story requests contain applicants' personal data, so they open only for the site owner. There is no shared password: a password written into a web page can be read by anyone from the page source.
